Welcome to my Trip Planner

When going on a trip, there are many things to plan. For long road trips and plane rides, what to do during the "waiting" periods is often the hardest thing to decide. With all of modern entertainment at our fingertips, choosing can be difficult. and making sure you don't choose material that is too long or too short is trickey

Find Movies and podcasts to fill the time

The first thing you need to do is choose what material you are interested in. Use our search tool to find material you might be interested in, and add it to your wishlist!

Manage your saved media list

Once you have prospective material, you re-order and remove your saved material here.

Plan Your Trip's Entertainment

Create a timeline of your trip, add stops and breaks, select material for the specific trip from your wishlist, and order it.

Venture Forth!

Once you have your itinerary, you can enjoy your trip without worrying about what to do during the long waits. Just follow your plan and enjoy the journey!
